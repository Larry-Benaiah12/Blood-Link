How it works

From request to donor

Three simple steps connect a hospital in need with the people nearby who can help.

  1. 01

    Donors register

    Blood group, phone number and rough location. It takes about a minute.

  2. 02

    Hospitals ask

    A hospital posts what it needs and how urgent it is.

  3. 03

    We match

    Compatible donors who haven't given blood in the last 90 days, closest first.
